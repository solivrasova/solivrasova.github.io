4 Vesting Schedule

To maintain long-term trust and market stability, locked tokens follow a phased release system:

  • Core Team (500M): 12-month cliff (no release) · Linear vesting over 24 months after cliff
  • Ecosystem & Partnership Allocation: Gradual quarterly release · Maximum 5% of total supply unlocked per quarter · Subject to strategic need and community transparency
  • Humanitarian Fund: Released in structured tranches · Transparent reporting model · On-chain verifiable wallet structure

This prevents sudden market flooding and protects price stability.
